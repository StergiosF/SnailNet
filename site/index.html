Your connection, shared out by percentage

Safe for your connection and your privacy

Steps aside instead of breaking things

If the service stops, packets flow straight through. After three failures in ten minutes it goes into Safe mode, and Pause stops all shaping in one click.

Nothing about you leaves your PC

No telemetry, no account, no analytics. SnailNet reads which app owns a connection and the website's name, never what you send or receive.

Download SnailNet for Windows

It adds a small Windows service and the WinDivert driver, and uninstalling removes both. Close the app and your rules keep working.

Up and running in three steps

  1. 1Download and run the installerIt asks Windows for permission once, to install the service.
  2. 2Choose More info, then Run anywayThe installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show “Windows protected your PC”.
  3. 3Set your speed and pick a modeSnailNet measures your connection in about 20 seconds, or you can type it.
